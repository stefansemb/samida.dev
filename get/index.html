Memory, plainly

It remembers you in files you can open yourself

No vector database, no opaque embeddings you have to trust. What SAMIDA remembers about you lives in Markdown files, routed by topic — edit them by hand, delete a line you don't like, and it takes effect on the next message.

The richer profiles that read this memory are limited to your own account. Anyone else who talks to your instance gets the same assistant, minus your personal context.
